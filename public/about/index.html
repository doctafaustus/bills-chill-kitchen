Bangers on a Budget #

Bangers on a Budget is my series about making impressive meals with inexpensive ingredients. The kind of plate you'd happily serve to guests, made from what's cheap at the grocery store.

  • Every recipe lists what it actually cost, including the share of pantry ingredients used.
  • Groceries come from H-E-B, so prices are real, not estimates.
  • Every recipe has a video so you can see exactly how it's made.

Note:Prices reflect my own shopping trips and will vary by store and week.
