Lab validation is essential for food safety, but labs test a small, fixed set of appliances. One leading lab bases its air fryer instructions on ten air fryers and notes the result isn't guaranteed for every design on the market. Your customers own hundreds of models.

The short version:Labs tell you the product is safe. We tell you whether people actually get it right at home, and what to change when they don't.

How it works

You send the product and the question. We handle recruiting, logistics, filming, and analysis.

  1. Brief us. Tell us the product, the instructions or claim you want tested, and the appliances that matter to you. A 30-minute call is enough.
  2. We match the cooks. We select 6 to 20 home cooks by appliance type, wattage, fuel, and household, so your results reflect the kitchens you sell into.
  3. They cook, unscripted. Each cook follows your package exactly as written, on camera, and records probe temperatures, times, and photos at every stage.
  4. You get answers. Within about two weeks you receive a findings report, a prioritized fix list, a highlight reel, and every raw session.

What you get

  • Findings report: success rate by appliance type, temperature and time data, failure points, and direct quotes.
  • Fix list: specific, prioritized changes to instructions, packaging, or product.
  • Confusion timestamps: the exact moments cooks hesitated, misread, or improvised.
  • Highlight reel: a short edit for internal reviews and stakeholder decks.
  • Raw sessions: every full recording, for your research team.
  • Licensable clips (optional): authentic first-cook moments, cleared for your marketing with each cook's written consent.

FAQ

Is this confidential?

Yes. Every cook signs an NDA, nothing is posted publicly, and all footage and findings belong to you.

What if the results are bad?

That's the point. It's much cheaper to find a problem with a dozen private cooks than with thousands of customers and a wave of one-star reviews.

Does this replace lab testing?

No. Accredited labs validate food safety. We complement that by showing how instructions and products perform in real home kitchens.

Can we use the footage in ads?

Yes, as an option. Clips are licensed with each cook's written consent, with clear terms for where and how long they run.

Do the cooks know which brand they're testing?

Your choice. We can run studies branded, or repackaged and blind when you want unbiased reactions.

How do products get to the cooks?

For products on shelves, cooks buy them at their local store and we reimburse them, which also tests the real retail product. For prototypes, we coordinate shipping.
