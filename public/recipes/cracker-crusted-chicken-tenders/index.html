Cracker-crusted chicken tenders

Fifteen years ago I wrote "try crackers instead of breadcrumbs" in my recipe spreadsheet. I finally tried it. Spoiler: crackers win.

↓ Jump to recipe
Serves2–3
Marinate1–4 hours
Oil350°F, about ¾ inch deep
Done when165°F in the thickest tender

Ingredients #

Tap an ingredient to check it off.

  • 1 lb chicken tenders
  • 1½ cups crushed buttery crackers (about 1 sleeve)
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup finely grated Parmesan
  • 1 lemon (zest of ½, plus a squeeze for finishing)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, divided, plus a pinch for finishing
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Oil for frying (about ¾ inch in the pan)

Method #

Marinate and crush

  1. Whisk the buttermilk, Dijon, garlic, ¾ tsp salt, and ½ tsp pepper. Add the chicken, make sure every piece is coated, then cover and refrigerate for 1–4 hours.
  2. Put the crackers in a zip-top bag and crush them fine with a rolling pin or heavy pan. Try not to leave any big chunks.

Set up the breading

  1. In a wide, shallow dish, mix 1½ cups cracker crumbs with the Parmesan, the zest of ½ lemon (just the yellow part), and the remaining ¼ tsp salt.
  2. For the egg wash, beat 1 egg with 1 tbsp of the marinade until smooth.

Bread and fry

  1. Let the extra marinade drip off each tender, dip it in the egg, then press it firmly into the crumbs on both sides. The more crumbs that stick, the crunchier they'll be.
  2. Heat about ¾ inch of oil to 350°F. Fry in batches for 3–4 minutes per side, flipping once, until deep golden brown.

Drain and finish

  1. Move them to a wire rack so the bottoms don't get soggy. Check that the thickest tender reaches at least 165°F.
  2. While they're still hot, finish with a pinch of salt and a light squeeze of lemon.

Serve with:Buttered corn, green beans, peas, or coleslaw.
