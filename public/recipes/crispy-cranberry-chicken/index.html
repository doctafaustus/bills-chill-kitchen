Crispy cranberry chicken with white bean purée

A dinner-party plate for four, about $9.96 total. A little fancy for two cans of beans and some chicken quarters.

Serves4
Total cost$9.96 (about $2.49 a plate)
Groceries fromH-E-B
Oven400°F
Done when175°F in the thickest part, away from the bone

Ingredients #

Tap an ingredient to check it off.

  • 4 chicken leg quarters, separated into thighs and drumsticks
  • 2 cans Great Northern beans, drained and rinsed
  • ½ cup plus 3 tbsp whole-berry cranberry sauce
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 small shallot, finely minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, plus zest for finishing
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp butter, divided
  • 2½ tbsp red wine vinegar, divided
  • 2 tbsp dark brown sugar
  • 1–2 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes, divided
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt, divided
  • ½ tsp black pepper, divided
  • Water as needed

Method #

Roast the chicken

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Pat the chicken dry. Use about 1 tsp salt and ¼ tsp black pepper to season the chicken, reserving the rest for the beans and sauce. Rub the drumsticks with ½ tbsp olive oil and roast on a lined baking sheet for around 35 minutes before glazing.
  2. Place the thighs skin-side down in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Let the fat render and the skin become golden and crisp, then transfer the skillet to the oven to finish cooking.

Glaze the drumsticks

  1. For the glaze, combine ½ cup cranberry sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1–2 tsp soy sauce, ½ tsp Dijon, ½ tbsp vinegar, ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, and a splash of water. Simmer for 3–5 minutes until glossy.
  2. Brush the drumsticks with glaze and roast for 3–4 minutes. Add a second coat and roast a few minutes more. Aim for 175°F in the thickest part of both the thighs and drumsticks, checking away from the bone.

Make the pan sauce

  1. Transfer the cooked thighs to a plate, skin-side up. Leave 1–2 tbsp rendered fat and the browned bits in the skillet. Over medium-low heat, cook the shallot for 1–2 minutes. Add ½ tsp red pepper flakes and the remaining 2 tbsp vinegar, scraping up the browned bits.
  2. Add 3 tbsp cranberry sauce and ⅓ cup water. Simmer until lightly syrupy. Remove from the heat and stir in 1 tbsp cold butter. Season with a little of the reserved salt and pepper.

Blend the white bean purée

  1. For the purée, gently cook the garlic in the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil for 4–5 minutes over medium-low heat without browning. Add the beans, ½ cup water, and remaining 2 tbsp butter. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  2. Blend with 1 tbsp lemon juice until smooth, adding warm water as needed. Season with the remaining salt, black pepper, and ¼ tsp red pepper flakes.

Plate it

  1. Spread the purée on each plate and add a thigh and drumstick. Spoon the pan sauce around the chicken, keeping it off the crispy skin. Finish with lemon zest.

Tip:Keep the pan sauce off the skin. That's what keeps the thighs crispy all the way to the table.
