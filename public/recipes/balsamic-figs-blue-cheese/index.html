2-minute balsamic figs with blue cheese

A gourmet snack in about two minutes, with no cooking. Adapted from Gordon Ramsay's Fast Food.

↓ Jump to recipe
MakesAbout 16 pieces (8 figs)
TimeAbout 2 minutes, no cooking
Best whenFigs are in season (late summer in the US)

Watch the video #

Ingredients #

Tap an ingredient to check it off.

  • 8 fresh Black Mission figs
  • Blue cheese crumbles
  • Balsamic glaze, or balsamic vinegar
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Method #

  1. Wash the figs and pat them dry.
  2. Cut each fig in half from stem to base. Halves give each piece a flat surface to hold the cheese; the original recipe uses quarters. Keep the stems on for looks, or trim them off.
  3. Top each half with small-to-medium blue cheese crumbles. Big crumbles roll off.
  4. Drizzle every fig with balsamic glaze.
  5. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and freshly ground black pepper. Serve right away.

Tip:A squeeze bottle makes the olive oil drizzle much easier to control.
