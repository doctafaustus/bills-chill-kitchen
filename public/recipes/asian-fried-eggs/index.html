Asian fried eggs

Crispy fried eggs with toasted sesame, hoisin, and quick-pickled scallions and chilies. Adapted from a Jamie Oliver recipe.

↓ Jump to recipe
Serves2
TimeAbout 10 minutes
PanLarge non-stick frying pan with a lid

Watch the video #

Ingredients #

Tap an ingredient to check it off.

  • 2 scallions
  • 1–2 fresh mixed-color chilies
  • 2 heaping tablespoons mixed raw sesame seeds
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • A splash of red wine vinegar

Method #

  1. Thinly slice the scallions and chilies on an angle. Put them in a bowl of ice water with a splash of red wine vinegar and set aside. They'll crisp up and mellow while you cook.
  2. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Toast the sesame seeds for about a minute, until lightly golden.
  3. Add 1 tablespoon of olive oil, crack in the eggs, cover the pan, and fry until the eggs are done the way you like them.
  4. Plate the eggs. If your hoisin is thick, thin it with a little water, then drizzle it over the eggs.
  5. Drain the scallions and chilies, scatter them over the eggs, break the yolks, and serve.

Tip:Serve one egg sesame-side up and one flipped, so you get the toasted seeds and the crispy edges in every bite.
